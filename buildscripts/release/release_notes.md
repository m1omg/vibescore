VibeScore @VERSION@: MuseScore Studio 4.7 for Android, iPhone, iPad and the web browser.

**Web:** <https://m1omg.github.io/vibescore/> (no installation).

**Android** (9 or later, 64-bit ARM): download `VibeScore-@VERSION@-android-arm64.apk` on the device and open it.
Android asks you to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.

**iPhone and iPad** (iOS 17 or later): `VibeScore-@VERSION@-ios-unsigned.ipa` is installed with a sideloading tool such
as SideStore or AltStore, which signs it for your device. See the
[README](https://github.com/m1omg/vibescore#sideloading-on-iphone-and-ipad).

What VibeScore can and cannot do, and how to use it on a touch screen: see the
[README](https://github.com/m1omg/vibescore#readme). Please report problems in the
[issues](https://github.com/m1omg/vibescore/issues).
